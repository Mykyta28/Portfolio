Junior Cybersecurity / System Administrator

I'm an IT professional with a strong interest in System Administration, Networking, and Cybersecurity. I enjoy configuring and maintaining systems, troubleshooting technical issues, and understanding how networks and infrastructure work together.

My hands-on experience includes Windows and Linux environments, system configuration, hardware deployment, networking, and IT infrastructure support. I've worked with routers, device provisioning, operating system and BIOS configuration, and hardware maintenance.

I'm currently pursuing an Associate degree in Cybersecurity and have earned CompTIA Network+ and CompTIA Security+ certifications, strengthening my knowledge of networking and cybersecurity.

I also have experience in web development and programming, including Python, JavaScript, React.js, Node.js, HTML/CSS, and databases. I use these skills to better understand systems, automate tasks, solve problems, and build practical solutions.

My goal is to continue growing in system administration, networking, and cybersecurity while helping maintain, secure, and improve modern IT environments.
